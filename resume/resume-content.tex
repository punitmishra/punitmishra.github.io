customer can lean on --- and I've done it at enterprise scale for 14+ years. As Senior ML \& AI Tech
Lead in SAP CX Data \& AI I \emph{lead a team of thirteen} while staying hands-on: I \textbf{architect

pipelines to \textbf{GPU serving on custom bare-metal H100 clusters}. Recently I brought \textbf{SAP's
Nova model into our natural-language-to-SQL stack} for cost control and model optionality, and
architected a \textbf{hardened internal-auth contract} that closes a cross-tenant data-leak class. I
care about the ``so what'': automated onboarding over manual tenant setup, latency cut on our flagship
retail deployment, security you can actually verify. Deep in \textbf{Python} and \textbf{Rust}, with
multi-model serving, distributed inference, vector search, and \textbf{secure, ethical AI}.
