\section{Profile}
I like taking machine learning the whole distance --- from rough prototype to something a real
customer can lean on --- and I've done it at enterprise scale for 14+ years, all of them at SAP
(joining through the CallidusCloud acquisition). As Senior ML \& AI Tech Lead in SAP CX Data \& AI I
\emph{lead a team of thirteen} while staying hands-on: I \textbf{architect the systems and also write,
deploy, and own them end to end}, from agent runtimes and LLM inference pipelines to \textbf{GPU serving
on custom bare-metal H100 clusters}. Recently I brought \textbf{SAP's own LLM (Nova)} into our
\textbf{natural-language-to-SQL} stack --- \textbf{lifting SQL accuracy} while cutting dependence on
external frontier models --- and architected a \textbf{hardened internal-auth contract} that closes a
cross-tenant data-leak class. I also architect \textbf{APEX}, our \textbf{Domain Data Access Agents}
platform. I care about the ``so what'': \textbf{measurable accuracy}, automated onboarding over manual
tenant setup, latency cut on our flagship retail deployment, security you can actually verify. Deep in
\textbf{Python} and \textbf{Rust}, with multi-model serving, distributed inference, vector search, and
\textbf{secure, ethical AI}.
